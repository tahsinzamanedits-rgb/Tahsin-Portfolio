FAQ

Quick answers.

How long does it take to get my video? +
Most edits are delivered within 48 hours. Bigger long-form projects usually take 3–5 days. You'll always get a clear timeline before we start.
What do you need from me to start? +
Just your raw footage (a Google Drive or Dropbox link works), the platform you're posting to, and the goal of the video. I take it from there.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes — every project includes revision rounds until the edit feels right. My goal is a video you're excited to publish, not one you settle for.
Can you handle both long-form and Shorts? +
Absolutely. I edit long-form YouTube videos and repurpose them into Shorts, Reels, and TikToks — one shoot, a full week of content.
